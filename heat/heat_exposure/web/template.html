Read the measures

Hot hours: one count for each valid hourly average strictly above the threshold. Equal temperatures do not count.

Degree-hours: sum of max(temperature − threshold, 0) for each valid hour. This combines duration with intensity; it is not energy.

Longest spell: longest uninterrupted run of hot hours. Missing or cooler hours break a run. It is a lower bound when data are missing.

Use the right conclusion

NASA POWER provides gridded, hourly average outdoor air temperature at 2 meters (T2M), not a thermometer at a facility intake. Coordinates are city reference points, not specific data centers.

One year is a weather snapshot, not a climate normal, design temperature or forecast. Cooling cost and reliability also depend on humidity, cooling equipment, facility load, water and electricity prices.
